What We Make, And How The Choice Is Decided

Which flange you need is decided by the joint, not by preference. What the flange is fixed to — butt-welded pipe, a socket, a thread, a nozzle to be closed off — picks the type. What the line carries and what it is bolting up against picks the drilling and the facing. Get those two right and the flange fits first time; get either wrong and it goes back on the lorry.

Everything on this page is machined in our own works at Darukhana, Mumbai. Blanks come in, and flanges go out turned, faced, drilled and marked, which is why a single odd flange to a drawing is as ordinary for us as a set of forty to a table. Send the specification page, a drawing, or a photograph of the flange already on the line and we will tell you what it is before quoting.

The standard grade is mild steel to IS:2062, which covers water, sewage, fire-fighting, compressed air, general works and structural service. Where the line carries something that would attack carbon steel, or where the client insists on stainless, we make the same range in SS 304, 304L, 316 and 316L. Tell us the grade at enquiry stage, because it moves the rate considerably.

The seven flange types we make, and where each belongs

  • Weld Neck Flanges — tapered hub butt-welded to the pipe; the pressure-line flange
  • Slip-On & Hub Flanges — slips over the pipe end and is fillet welded; quick fit-up
  • Blind Flanges — no bore; closes a line, a nozzle or a spare tee
  • Socket Weld Flanges — pipe seats in a machined socket; small-bore work to 80mm NB
  • Male & Female Flanges — spigot and recess pair, including tongue and groove
  • Forged Flanges — blank forged rather than plate-cut, where the specification asks for it
  • Table D / E / F / H Flanges — the BS 10 lettered drilling used across Indian works

M.S. Flanges – Frequently Asked Questions

What are M.S. Flanges?

+

M.S. (Mild Steel) Flanges are flat disc-shaped pipe fittings manufactured from IS:2062 grade mild steel. They are bolted in pairs — with a gasket between their mating faces — to create a pressure-tight, leak-proof and easily dismantlable connection between pipes, valves, pumps and equipment in industrial piping systems.

What standards are M.S. Flanges manufactured to?

+

I.S. Engineering Co. manufactures M.S. Flanges to: IS:1538 Table E (E Table Flanges), IS:6392 (Flat Face Flanges), ANSI B16.5 (Welding Neck, Blind, Slip-On flanges), ANSI B16.11 (Socket Weld), BS 4504 (British Standard) and customer-specific drawings. Material conforms to IS:2062.

What is the size range of M.S. Flanges available?

+

I.S. Engineering Co. manufactures M.S. Flanges in sizes from 15mm NB (½ inch) to 600mm NB (24 inch). Custom sizes outside this range are manufactured on request. All standard NB sizes per IS, ANSI and BS specifications are available.

What face types are available in M.S. Flanges?

+

M.S. Flanges are available in: Flat Face (FF) — for IS:1538 E Table Flanges and water service; Raised Face (RF) — for ANSI B16.5 flanges in oil & gas; Ring Joint Face (RTJ/RJ) — for very high pressure critical service. The face type must match the mating flange and gasket type.

What is the difference between Flat Face and Raised Face flanges?

+

Flat Face (FF) flanges have a completely flat mating surface and require a full-face gasket covering the entire flange face. Used with IS:1538 E Table Flanges in water, sewage and low-medium pressure service. Raised Face (RF) flanges have a small raised annular ring around the bore where the gasket seats. Used with ANSI B16.5 flanges in oil & gas and higher-pressure process piping.

How do I select the correct M.S. Flange type?

+

Select based on: (1) Standard — IS:1538 for Indian projects, ANSI B16.5 for international; (2) Flange Type — Blind for termination, Weld Neck for high pressure, Slip-On for low pressure; (3) Pressure Class — PN6/PN10/PN16 for IS, Class 150–2500 for ANSI; (4) Face Type — FF for IS standard, RF for ANSI; (5) Size — match pipe NB.
